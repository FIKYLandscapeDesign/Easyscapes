About Us

We make great landscape design accessible to everyone.

At easyscapes, we make great landscape design accessible to everyone. Our collection of professionally designed landscape templates offers a simple, affordable alternative to traditional custom design services.

Beautiful landscapes. Simplified.

Our templates have been created by a professional landscape designer with years of experience across residential new builds. Each template reflects a distinct style, carefully curated with plant selections, materials and layouts that work beautifully together.

Rather than spending $3,000 to $10,000 on a custom landscape architect, easyscapes gives you a professionally designed starting point at a fraction of the cost.

Vision

To become Australia's leading destination for affordable landscape design templates for new home builders.

Mission

To simplify landscape design by providing professionally curated, affordable templates that make great design accessible to everyone.

Who We Are For

New home builders, DIY homeowners, and landscapers who need a professional design starting point without the custom price tag.
