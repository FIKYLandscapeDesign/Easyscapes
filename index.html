About

Simple design. Accessible to everyone.

easyscapes makes great landscape design accessible to everyone. We combine professional landscape expertise with easy, ready-to-use templates that take the guesswork, and high design costs, out of creating your dream front yard.

Designed for frontages 6m to 20m wide, simply choose the landscape style you love and upload a photo of your home.

Within one business day, you'll receive a professionally designed landscape template, personalised to suit your home and façade, complete with a visualisation showing how your new front garden could look, before you plant a thing.

Your home. Your style. See your future landscape come to life.

How it works

01

Choose your style
