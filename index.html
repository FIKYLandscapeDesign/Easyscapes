FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot.

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

What is included in each template?

+
Every template includes a moodboard, 2D landscape plan (front yard), plant plan with species recommendations, a 3D render of the finished design, and a maintenance guide.

Will the template fit my block?

+
Templates are drawn to a standard 12.5m frontage by 7m depth. They can usually be adapted to blocks within about 2 to 3 metres of that size. If your block is very different in size or shape, our sister studio FIKY Landscape Design can help with a custom solution.

Can I change the plants?

+
Yes. The templates are guides, not rigid plans. Plant selections, materials and layouts can all be adjusted to suit your site, soil type, climate, budget and personal taste. The overall design structure stays intact to keep things looking great.

How do I get my design after I pay?

+
After purchase you will receive an email with a download link to your full design package. Everything is delivered as high-quality PDF files, ready to use straight away or share with your landscaper.

Do the templates cover the backyard?

+
Templates are currently designed for the front yard. A backyard pack upgrade is available for selected templates. For a full custom front and back design, visit FIKY Landscape Design.

Can I give the plan to a landscaper?

+
Absolutely. The templates are designed to hand directly to any landscaper. The 2D plan, plant list and 3D render give them everything they need to quote and build the job.

Can I do the work myself?

+
Yes. Each template is designed with DIY in mind. Clear planting layouts, a full plant list and visual guidance make it easy to tackle yourself at whatever pace suits your budget.

What if none of the templates suit me?

+
If a template is not quite right for your site or vision, our sister studio FIKY Landscape Design offers fully custom residential landscape design. Visit fikylandscapedesign.com to enquire.

What does the maintenance score mean?

+
Every template includes a Low to High maintenance rating. Low means minimal pruning, watering and upkeep — great for busy households. Medium means occasional seasonal care. High means regular pruning, shaping and planting, ideal for keen gardeners.

Are the templates designed for South Australia?

+
The templates are designed with South Australian conditions in mind but most styles work across temperate Australian climates. If you are unsure about specific plant suitability for your area, we recommend checking with a local nursery before purchasing.
