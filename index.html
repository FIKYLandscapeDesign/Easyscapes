About

Great design.
Accessible to everyone.

easyscapes makes great landscape design accessible to everyone. We combine professional landscape expertise with beautiful, ready-to-use templates that take the guesswork out of creating your dream outdoor space.

Our templates are designed to work for blocks ranging between 8m and 15m wide — covering the vast majority of Australian front yards.
