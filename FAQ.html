Faq

Questions we get asked a lot

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

What is included in each template?

+
Every template includes a moodboard, plant plan with species recommendations, and a 3D render of the finished design. All delivered as PDF files instantly after purchase.

Will the template fit my block?

+
Our templates are designed to work for blocks ranging between 8m and 15m wide. If your block falls outside this range, get in touch and we can advise.

How do i pay?

+
We use Stripe for secure payment processing. When you click purchase, you'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout page to enter your payment details. We accept all major credit and debit cards. You'll never enter card details on the easyscapes site directly.

How do i get my design after i pay?

+
After purchase you'll receive an email with a download link to your full design package as high-quality PDF files, ready to use straight away.

Can i change the plants?

+
Yes. The templates are guides, not rigid plans. Plant selections, materials and layouts can all be adjusted to suit your site, soil type, climate and budget.

Can i give the plan to a landscaper?

+
Absolutely. The templates are designed to hand directly to any landscaper. The plant plan and 3D render give them everything they need to quote and build the job.

Can i do the work myself?

+
Yes. Each template is designed with DIY in mind. Clear planting layouts and visual guidance make it easy to tackle yourself at whatever pace suits your budget.

What does the maintenance score mean?

+
Low means minimal pruning and watering — great for busy households. Medium means occasional seasonal care.

What is the landscape visualiser?

+
The landscape visualiser lets you optionally upload a photo of your home and see how your chosen design could look on your actual property. It is currently in development and coming soon.

Do you do backyard designs?

+
We're currently focused on front yard designs. If you're interested in a combined front and backyard package, email us at hello@easyscapes.com and we'd love to discuss it.
